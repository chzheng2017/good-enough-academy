# Good Enough Academy

> **The Philosophy**: You don't need 10,000 hours to master a skill. With focused, deliberate practice over just **20 hours**, you can become *good enough* to outperform 80% of casual practitioners. This academy is built on that principle.

## What This Is

A collection of **practical, AI-generated learning paths** for skills you want to pick up quickly. Each course is designed to get you from zero to "genuinely useful" in a few weeks of casual practice—not years of study.

The lessons are:
- **Short and focused** — each lesson teaches one specific thing you can use immediately
- **Practical over theoretical** — no fluff, no academic depth unless it directly helps you
- **AI-assisted** — generated with careful prompts to match your learning style and goals
- **Structured for 20-hour learning** — roughly 1-2 hours per week for 10-20 weeks

## Courses

### 🎵 [Lyric Writing](./lyric-writing/)
Learn to write lyrics for pop, rock, and R&B songs. Perfect if you're making music with AI tools (like Suno) and want to move beyond generic AI-generated lyrics.

**What you'll learn:**
- Core concepts: prosody, rhyme schemes, show-don't-tell
- How to diagnose why a lyric isn't working
- How to write a complete song (verse, chorus, bridge)
- How to edit and improve AI-generated drafts

**Time commitment:** ~20 hours over 8-10 weeks

### 🎬 [Making Great YouTube Videos with AI Tools](./ai-youtube-videos/)
Build an efficient AI-powered video production workflow. Learn which tools to use at each stage—from scripting and voiceover to editing, thumbnails, and SEO.

**What you'll learn:**
- AI tools for ideation, scripting, and voiceover generation
- Video editing and visual effects with AI
- Thumbnail and thumbnail generation
- Building a repeatable workflow for consistent publishing

**Time commitment:** ~20 hours over 2-4 weeks

## How to Use This Academy

1. **Pick a course** that matches something you want to learn
2. **Follow the lessons in order** — they build on each other
3. **Do the practice exercises** — that's where the learning happens
4. **Track your progress** — each course has a learning record to document what you've tried

Each course folder contains:
- `MISSION.md` — why you're learning this and what success looks like
- `NOTES.md` — learning preferences and tips for getting the most out of the lessons
- `RESOURCES.md` — links to tools, references, and inspiration
- `learning-records/` — numbered lessons with exercises and examples

## Philosophy

This academy rejects the myth of the 10,000-hour rule. Research shows that **deliberate practice in focused bursts** is far more effective than passive accumulation of hours. The goal isn't mastery—it's competence. It's being good enough to:

- Create things you're proud of
- Understand the craft well enough to improve
- Compete with casual practitioners
- Have fun while learning

If you want to go deeper later, you can. But you don't need to. 20 hours is enough.

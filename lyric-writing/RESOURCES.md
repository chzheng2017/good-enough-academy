# Resources

## Primary (verified, high-quality)

### Courses

- **[Songwriting: Writing the Lyrics](https://www.coursera.org/learn/songwriting-lyrics)** — Pat Pattison (Berklee / Coursera). Free to audit. 4 modules: song development, prosody (stability/instability), rhyme schemes, and language rhythm. 4.8/5 stars, 148K+ enrolled. The single best structured resource for learning lyric craft from scratch. Pattison literally wrote the book.

### Books

- **Writing Better Lyrics** by Pat Pattison (2nd ed., Writer's Digest Books, 2009). Recommended by John Mayer and Ross Copperman. Covers object writing, prosody, rhyme types, song structure. The companion text to the Coursera course.

### Articles & Guides

- **[How to Write Great Song Lyrics in 5 Steps](https://blog.landr.com/how-to-write-lyrics/)** — LANDR Blog. Beginner-friendly overview: journaling, melody-first vs. lyric-first, building narrative, working section by section.

## Communities (for wisdom)

- **[r/Songwriting](https://www.reddit.com/r/Songwriting/)** — Active subreddit for feedback, craft discussion, and lyric critique.
- **[Songwriting Pro](https://www.songwritingpro.com/)** — Online community with peer song reviews (paid, 14-day free trial).

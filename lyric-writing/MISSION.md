# Mission: Lyric Writing

## Why

I'm making AI-generated music with Suno as a hobby/experiment. The music generation is good, but AI-written lyrics feel generic and unsatisfying. I want to write my own lyrics — or at least be skilled enough to heavily direct and edit AI drafts — so the words match the quality of the sound.

## Genres

Pop / synth-pop, rock / alt-rock / indie, R&B / soul. These share verse-chorus structure, emotional directness, and hook-driven writing, but differ in tone, rhythm, and lyrical convention.

## Success looks like

- I can write a complete set of lyrics (verse, chorus, bridge) that I'm proud to feed into Suno.
- The lyrics sound natural when sung — not forced, not generic.
- I understand enough craft to diagnose *why* a lyric isn't working and fix it.
- I can edit AI-generated drafts with confidence, keeping what works and rewriting what doesn't.

## Constraints

- This is a hobby, not a career path. Lessons should be practical and fun, not academic.
- I'm generating the music with AI, so I don't need to learn melody writing or music theory — just the words.
- Time investment: casual. Short, focused lessons that each give me something I can immediately use.

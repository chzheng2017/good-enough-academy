# Learning Record 005: Writing Your First Song

**Date:** 2026-09-11
**Lesson:** [005 — Writing Your First Song](../lessons/0005-writing-your-first-song.html)

## What was learned

- The **8-step songwriting process**: seed → sense-mining → box diagram (journey map) → structure → chorus first → verse → bridge → audit with four levers.
- **Write the chorus first** — it's the destination. If you don't know where you're going, the verses wander.
- The **development engine** (Pattison's "Boxes"): map each section's role in one sentence before writing any lyrics. V1 = situation, Chorus = core truth, V2 = complication, Bridge = turn.
- **Structure template** for first songs: V1 (5–6 lines, unstable) → Chorus (4 lines, stable) → V2 → Chorus → Bridge (3–4 lines, different) → Final Chorus.
- **Audit checklist**: content (showing?), line count (contrast?), line length (surprise?), rhyme (verse looser than chorus?).
- **Format for Suno**: section labels in square brackets, iterate by generating and listening.
- Walked through a complete example ("porch light" song) from seed to finished lyrics with four-lever audit.

## Skills practiced

- The full creation process from blank page to complete song.
- Applying all four prosody levers as quality control on a draft.

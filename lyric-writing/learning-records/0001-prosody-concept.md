# Learning Record 001: Prosody — The Central Concept

**Date:** 2026-09-11
**Lesson:** [001 — Why AI Lyrics Fall Flat](../lessons/0001-why-ai-lyrics-fall-flat.html)

## What was learned

- **Prosody** is the match between all song elements and the central emotion. It's the #1 diagnostic concept for evaluating lyrics.
- AI lyrics break prosody because they optimize for surface patterns (rhyme, syllable count, structure) without understanding the song's emotional intent.
- Four prosody levers: number of lines, line length, rhyme scheme, rhythm (stress pattern).
- Showing (concrete images, actions) vs. telling (labeling emotions) — showing is almost always stronger.

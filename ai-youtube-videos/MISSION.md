# Mission: Making Great YouTube Videos with AI Tools

## Why I'm Learning This

I want to create a YouTube channel that experiments with AI tools in daily life, then explains the tech behind them. Most people only use AI for basic Q&A — I want to show them how to do more, and as an engineer, I can go deeper into the tech behind the scenes. This requires me to produce polished, engaging videos efficiently using AI-powered workflows.

## What I Already Know

- Experienced with traditional video production (filming, editing, publishing)
- Strong engineering background — can understand and explain AI concepts
- New to AI-powered video production workflows specifically
- Familiar with AI tools as a user, not yet as a video production tool

## What I Want To Be Able To Do

1. Use AI tools across the entire video production pipeline — from ideation and scripting to editing, thumbnails, music, and SEO
2. Publish my first AI-assisted video within 1-2 weeks
3. Build a repeatable, efficient AI workflow over 1-2 months for consistent publishing
4. Understand which AI tools are best for which stage, and when to use human judgment instead
5. Create content that demonstrates AI experiments in daily life with clear, engineer-level tech explanations

## Scope

### In Scope
- AI tools for every stage of YouTube production: ideation, scripting, voiceover, video generation, editing, thumbnails, music, captions, SEO, repurposing
- Evaluating and comparing tools (free vs paid, quality vs speed)
- Building a personal AI video workflow
- Understanding the tech behind the tools (enough to explain on camera)

### Out of Scope
- Traditional filmmaking techniques (already known)
- Building AI models from scratch
- Monetization strategy and YouTube business growth (separate topic)

## Time Horizon

- First AI-assisted video: 1-2 weeks
- Full AI workflow established: 1-2 months
- Ongoing: continuous learning as new tools emerge

# Resources: Making Great YouTube Videos with AI Tools

## Knowledge

### AI Video Generation & Editing
- [Runway](https://runwayml.com/) — General World Models for video generation; Gen-4.5 model for text/image-to-video; creative suite with editing tools. Used by major studios.
- [Descript](https://www.descript.com/) — Text-based AI video editor. Edit video by editing transcript. Features: Studio Sound (noise removal), eye contact correction, filler word removal, AI speech regeneration, green screen, auto captions, clip creation. Free tier available.
- [Adobe Premiere Pro AI](https://www.adobe.com/products/premiere/ai-video-editing.html) — Professional editor with AI features: Object Mask, AI-powered color, audio enhancement. Industry standard for serious editing.
- [InVideo AI](https://invideo.io/) — AI video platform with Agent Two (frontier creative AI). Text-to-video, AI avatars, storyboarding, timeline editor. Access to models: Seedance, Veo 3.1, Kling 3. Multiplayer collaboration.
- [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/) — Free AI-powered video editor by ByteDance. Popular for short-form content, auto captions, effects. (Site was unavailable during research — verify current features)

### AI Avatars & Talking Head Generation
- [HeyGen](https://www.heygen.com/) — #1 rated AI avatar platform (G2). Avatar V model for lifelike digital twins. Features: Video Agent (prompt-to-video), lip-sync in 175+ languages, AI Studio text-based editor, video translation. Used by Coursera, Fortune 100.
- [Synthesia](https://www.synthesia.io/) — AI video platform for business. 240+ avatars, 1000+ AI voices, 160+ languages. SOC 2, ISO 42001, GDPR compliant. Strong in L&D and enterprise.

### AI Music & Audio
- [Suno](https://suno.com/) — AI music generator. Create complete songs (vocals, lyrics, production) from text prompts. 10 free songs/day. Pro plan: 500 songs/month with commercial rights, stem export for DAWs. Top 10 music app on iOS/Android.
- [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/) — AI voice synthesis and cloning. Industry leader for realistic voiceovers. (Site unavailable during research — verify features independently)

### AI Image Generation (Thumbnails, B-Roll Stills)
- [Midjourney](https://www.midjourney.com/) — Leading AI image generator known for artistic quality. Useful for thumbnails, concept art, visual brainstorming. (Site blocked during research — verify independently)
- [Canva AI](https://www.canva.com/) — Design platform with AI image generation, Magic Studio tools. Accessible for non-designers. Good for thumbnails and social graphics. (Site blocked during research — verify independently)
- [Leonardo.ai](https://leonardo.ai/) — AI image generation with fine control. Good for consistent visual styles. (Site unavailable during research — verify independently)
- [DALL-E / ChatGPT Image Generation](https://openai.com/) — OpenAI's image generation integrated into ChatGPT. Quick and accessible.

### AI for YouTube SEO & Optimization
- [VidIQ](https://vidiq.com/) — YouTube SEO and analytics tool with AI features for keyword research, title/description optimization, and trend analysis. (Site rate-limited during research — verify independently)
- [TubeBuddy](https://www.tubebuddy.com/) — Browser extension for YouTube optimization, A/B testing thumbnails, SEO suggestions.

### AI Clipping & Repurposing
- [OpusClip](https://opus.pro/) — #1 AI video clipping tool. Turns long videos into short-form clips. Features: ClipAnything (works on any genre), AI reframe, AI captions, AI B-roll, social scheduler. Used by 16M+ creators including MrBeast, Logan Paul.

### AI Scriptwriting & Ideation

- [Claude](https://claude.ai/) — Alternative AI assistant. Strong at long-form writing, analysis, and structured content.
- [Google Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/) — Google's AI with deep YouTube/Search integration for trend research.

Minimum Viable AI Workflow (First Video)

  1. Ideate: ChatGPT → 10 video ideas
  2. Script: Claude/ChatGPT → outline → draft → your rewrite
  3. Record: Your camera + screen recording (this is the unique content)
  4. Edit: Descript (text-based) or your existing editor + AI audio cleanup
  5. Thumbnail: DALL-E/Midjourney background + your face photo
  6. Publish: ChatGPT → description, tags, timestamps
  7. Repurpose: OpusClip → Shorts from the published video
